STOCKWISE AI 
AI-Powered Inventory Management System
1. Project Overview
StockWise AI is a web-based inventory management application designed to help small businesses efficiently manage products, monitor stock levels, track inventory transactions, and make informed purchasing decisions.
Traditional inventory management often relies on manual records and spreadsheets, making it difficult to identify low-stock products, monitor inventory movement, and maintain accurate stock information.
StockWise AI addresses these challenges through a centralized digital platform integrated with Artificial Intelligence (AI). It provides real-time stock monitoring, automated low-stock alerts, inventory analytics, intelligent restocking recommendations, and an AI-powered chatbot.
The application aims to simplify inventory operations, reduce manual effort, improve stock accuracy, and support data-driven business decisions.

3. Project Objectives
The primary objective of StockWise AI is to develop an intelligent inventory management system that improves stock visibility, operational efficiency, and inventory decision-making.
Specific Objectives
1. Centralized Inventory Management: Maintain product, supplier, and inventory information in a centralized database.
2. Real-Time Stock Tracking: Automatically update product quantities whenever stock-in or stock-out transactions occur.
3. Low-Stock Monitoring: Identify products that reach or fall below their predefined reorder levels.
4. Transaction Management: Maintain accurate records of all inventory movements.
5. Inventory Analytics: Display inventory statistics, stock movement trends, and performance reports.
6. AI-Based Inventory Analysis: Analyse inventory data to identify fast-moving, slow-moving, and potentially overstocked products.
7. Intelligent Restocking Recommendations: Suggest products that require replenishment using stock levels, sales history, and relevant inventory information.
8. AI Chatbot: Allow users to ask questions about inventory through a conversational interface.
9. Inventory Summaries: Generate understandable summaries of stock status and inventory performance.
10. Data Security: Protect inventory information through authentication and database access controls.

5. Project Scope (Out-of-Scope)
The following features are excluded from the initial MVP and may be considered for future development.
1. Multi-Branch Management: Managing inventory across multiple store locations or warehouses.
2. Advanced Billing and POS: Generating customer invoices, processing payments, and managing checkout operations.
3. Accounting Integration: Complete bookkeeping, tax management, payroll, and financial accounting.
4. Automated Supplier Purchasing: Automatically placing purchase orders with suppliers without admin involvement.
5. Barcode and QR Code Hardware Integration: Scanning physical product barcodes using dedicated hardware.
6. +Delivery and Logistics Management: Tracking shipments, delivery vehicles, and order fulfilment.
7. Customer Relationship Management: Managing customer profiles, loyalty programs, and marketing campaigns.
8. Advanced Demand Forecasting: Complex predictive forecasting using extensive historical sales data and external market information.
9. Mobile Native Application: Separate Android and iOS applications. The initial release will be a responsive web application.
10. Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform: Managing independent businesses, subscriptions, and billing within one platform.
6. Expected Project Outcome
The expected outcome is a functional, responsive, AI-powered inventory management web application that enables small businesses to manage their inventory efficiently.
The system will provide accurate stock tracking, streamlined inventory operations, automated low-stock alerts, visual reports, and AI-assisted inventory insights.
StockWise AI aims to reduce manual inventory management efforts, improve stock visibility, minimize avoidable stock shortages, and support better business decisions.
